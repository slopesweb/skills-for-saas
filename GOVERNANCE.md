# Governance

## Model

Skills for SaaS uses a maintainer-led, community-reviewed model.

## Roles

### Contributor

Opens issues, proposes skills, improves evaluations, and reviews changes.

### Reviewer

Provides domain review and may approve changes within documented expertise.

### Maintainer

Merges changes, manages releases, protects schemas and security policy, and appoints reviewers.

### Technical Steering Group

The project may establish a steering group after it has at least five active maintainers from at least three independent organizations.

## Decisions

- Routine changes use pull-request consensus.
- Public schema, licensing, compatibility, or governance changes require a written proposal.
- Maintainers seek consensus first.
- If consensus fails, active maintainers vote by simple majority.
- Security maintainers may apply temporary protective changes immediately and document them afterward.

## Conflicts of interest

Reviewers must disclose material commercial or organizational conflicts related to a decision.

## Inactivity

Maintainers inactive for six months may move to emeritus status after notice. Access may be removed immediately for compromised accounts or serious policy violations.
